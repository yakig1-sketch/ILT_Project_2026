How to test (about 2 minutes)

  1. Draw continuous loops for ~5 seconds without lifting. Repeat 3–4 times.
  2. Draw while pressing from very light to very hard.
  3. Hold the pen ~5 mm above the screen and move it without touching.
  4. Lean the pen left, right, forward and back while drawing.
  5. Lay a real ruler on the on-screen ruler and adjust until 50 mm match, then tap Ruler matches.
  6. Tap Download results and send the file.
