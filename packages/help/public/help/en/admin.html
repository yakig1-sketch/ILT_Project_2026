System Administrator guide

For the System Administrator: users, Domains and Regions, settings, the canvas library and reference recordings, cameras, ethics approvals, legacy data and the action log.

Your work in the system

You work in the Administrator cockpit, a separate address for the System Administrator only. Its tabs are Users, Domains, Regions, Canvases, Cameras, Settings, Ethics approvals, Legacy data and Action log. The address always names the open tab, for example …/admin#/settings.

  • Only you can change settings and canvases.
  • Changes to clinically governed values (scoring, timers, activating a canvas) also need a Research Director's email and PIN, typed on your screen when you save, while director approval is on (the default).
  • Every change is written to the action log, with the old and new values. Nothing in the log can be edited or deleted.
  • A new session takes a snapshot of the settings in effect. Changes apply to sessions created afterwards, never to existing ones.

Signing in

Open the Administrator cockpit address (the cockpit address followed by /admin, for example https://<server>:5174/admin) and sign in. The System Administrator can't sign in at the evaluator address, and other users can't sign in here: each is told the right address.

After 5 wrong passwords an account is locked for 15 minutes. The Help button on every tab opens the matching part of this guide.

Users

The list shows active users with their name, email, role, Domain, Region and status. Search (name or email), Role and Status (Active, Inactive, All) narrow it.

Roles

RoleUses
System AdministratorThe Administrator cockpit
ResearcherThe Evaluator cockpit and the analyst portal; with a director PIN, the Research Director
EvaluatorThe Evaluator cockpit: participants and sessions
AnalystThe analyst portal: de-identified research data only

Create a user

  1. + New user. Fill in the name, email, role, interface language, Domain and Region.
  2. A 16-character password is generated; Generate makes another. Copy it before you press Create and give it to the user in person. There is no email service.
  3. For a Researcher, an optional Research Director PIN (6 digits) makes them the Research Director (a green badge in the list).

Edit, unlock, reset, deactivate

  • Edit changes the name, role, language, Domain, Region and status. The email can't change (create a new user instead). Leave the password empty to keep it, or Generate a new one: a reset also unlocks the account.
  • A locked account (amber Locked) is opened again with Unlock.
  • PIN: leave it empty to keep it; tick Remove the Research Director PIN to remove it. Changing the role away from Researcher removes the PIN.
  • Deactivate (in the list, after a confirmation) stops the user from signing in; Reactivate brings them back. Users are never deleted.
  • A role change or deactivation reaches a user who is signed in within 15 minutes.
  • You can't deactivate yourself or change your own role.

Domains

A Domain groups students for ILT testing, for example a study group, a program or a population. It is not necessarily geographic. Every participant belongs to one Domain. Scoring settings and the language configuration can have their own values per Domain.

  • + New Domain: a code (letters, digits and underscores; saved in capitals, never changed), the Hebrew and English names, and descriptions.
  • Edit changes the names and descriptions.
  • Deactivate stops offering the Domain for new participants and users; existing ones keep it. At least one Domain must stay active.

Regions

A Region is a geographic area, for example an education district. It is optional for a participant. ILT-process settings (timers, warm-up, videos, templates) can have their own values per Region. Regions are created, renamed and deactivated like Domains; every Region may be inactive.

Settings

Choose a level: System, a Domain or a Region.

  • System shows every group: Scoring, ILT process, Languages, and Governance, privacy, device and log.
  • Domain shows Scoring and Languages; Region shows the ILT-process settings. A row with no value of its own says "Follows System" (or "Follows default"); Set own value gives it one, and Follow System removes it.
  • Point at a setting's description to see its default and allowed range.

Changing and saving

  1. Change the values. Changed rows are highlighted, with Undo and Back to default; a bar at the bottom counts the changes.
  2. Review and save lists every change, before and after. Add a reason if you like.
  3. If a change Needs a Research Director PIN, the Research Director types their email and PIN. One approval covers all the changes.
  4. Save. A value outside its range is refused and shown in red. The three composite weights must total 100%.

History lists the changes at the chosen level, newest first: when, the setting, before and after, who changed it, who approved it and the reason.

Settings to know

  • Pass/fail threshold on the composite score and the three weights in the composite (speed 50%, accuracy 25%, pen lifts 25% by default). Until the threshold is set, Pass/Fail shows "not configured".
  • Timers: Inactivity before the cue (20 s), Inactivity after the last box (30 s), Further time before the amber alert (10 s) and the Transition countdown before a block.
  • Warm-up block on or off, per Region.
  • Languages: the enabled languages and the default language; per language the ILT, notebook and letter canvases, the fill direction and whether the block is mirrored. A note warns when a canvas's recorded fill order differs from the fill direction.
  • Allow research sessions (after ethics approval) (see Ethics approvals), Hand video recording (subject to consent) (per Region), Clinical changes need the research director PIN (turning it off always needs a director), and the intervention settings (Training sheets in an intervention session, Days from training to the retention check, Show the row-3 similarity hint).

The canvas library

A canvas is the page the child writes on: an ILT block, a notebook page or an intervention letter sheet. Canvases are grouped by language → canvas set → canvas. Filter by Language and Type.

  • + New canvas set: a language, a type (ILT blocks, notebook pages, intervention letters) and names. Sets can be renamed and deactivated.
  • + Load a canvas: a set, a code (for example ILT_3X5_HE), names, the geometry as JSON (typed or Read from a file) and optionally a scoring profile. It is loaded as a Draft.
  • Each canvas has a status: Draft (editable) → Active (in use, never changed) → Retired.
  • Settings name a canvas by its code; new sessions get the Active version of that code.

Don't retire or replace the canvases in use (for example ILT_3X5_HE, ILT_3X5_EN, NOTEBOOK_HE, NOTEBOOK_EN) without planning it: every session uses them.

A canvas: preview, profile, versions

  • Preview: the houses and dots. Box numbers and fill order shows the box ids and the writing order. The size can be true size (exact on a 96 dpi screen), twice true size or fitted to the window. The true size on the tablet is checked in reference mode.
  • Scoring profile (ILT and letter canvases): the dot order (for example A → B → C), and per dot the hit radius (mm), the accuracy weight (%) and whether it is required. Weights total 100%. Also the expected pen lifts per block, the box fill order, the normal speed (mm/s) and the flexibility (± %).
  • A Draft can be edited (geometry, profile, names), checked (Check lists what must be fixed) and deleted (unless it has reference recordings). Changes are saved with Save draft.
  • Activate: needs no problems and, with director approval on, the Research Director's email and PIN. New sessions using the code then get this version.
  • New version makes an editable Draft copy of an Active canvas; activating it retires the previous version. Each canvas has at most one Draft.
  • Domain overrides: a Domain can have its own scoring values for an Active canvas (approved with the director's PIN). Changed values are highlighted; Follow the canvas profile removes them. Overrides carry over to new versions.
  • Retire is refused while a setting still names the canvas.
  • "Used in N session steps" and "Named in Settings" show where the canvas is used.

Reference recordings on the tablet

Some profile values come from samples drawn on a tablet in reference mode:

  1. On a canvas, press Pair a tablet in reference mode. Type the 6-digit code on the tablet. It shows the amber bar "Reference recording (not a child session)". The tablet stays paired while you open other canvases.
  2. Show on the tablet at true size: check the size with a ruler (an ILT house is 35 × 25 mm), then tap Done. Nothing is recorded.
  3. Record a sample block: fill every house in the order a child should, then Done. The cockpit shows the fill order and the pen lifts. Accept saves them into the profile; Clear discards the sample (it is kept for audit). An incomplete sample can only be cleared.
  4. Record the normal speed: a skilled writer writes one row (or the chosen rows) at a natural pace, then Done. The speed is computed with the same formula as for children. Accept stores it as the Normal speed.
  5. Disconnect the tablet when done; it returns to its code screen.
  • Record for: on a Draft, the recording goes into the canvas's own profile. On an Active canvas, choose a Domain: the value becomes that Domain's own, and Accept needs the director's PIN.
  • Until a normal speed is recorded, sessions show the speed score and composite as "not configured". Sessions created after the canvas has a normal speed get them.
  • Recordings of this canvas lists every recording: when, what, for whom, values, status, recorded by and decided by.

Cameras

Desk cameras record the child's writing hand. The server finds the ONVIF cameras on its network by itself; nobody types an address.

  1. In each camera's own app, set up a camera account (user name and password), the same on every camera.
  2. On Cameras, enter it under Site camera account and Save. The cameras are checked; each becomes Ready. The password is stored encrypted and never shown again.
  3. Search the network looks for new cameras.
  • Each camera shows its status (Ready, No account, Wrong account, Not answering), whether it can pan and tilt, when it was last seen, and Picture (a current picture).
  • Rename gives it a name (Enter saves, Escape cancels), for example "Camera 2".
  • Own account gives one camera its own account; Use the site account returns to the site account.
  • Retire stops using a camera for pairing; Restore brings it back.
  • Recording also needs the setting Hand video recording (subject to consent) and the participant's consent. Evaluators pair a camera to a session by a QR code on the tablet.
  • A warning at the top means the camera relay is not answering: cameras can't be checked, paired or recorded until it runs.

Ethics approvals

The approvals under which sessions run: their kind (for example Intervention programme or Research), the authority, the reference and the dates they are valid.

  • + Add an approval records one. "Valid from" must not be after "Valid until".
  • Withdraw keeps it in the list as withdrawn; Restore brings it back.
  • Each approval shows how many sessions used it.

Research sessions can be created only when the setting Allow research sessions (after ethics approval) is on, a Research approval is valid today, and the participant's consent allows research use. The top of this tab says whether research sessions are turned on.

Legacy data

Imports results from the previous application's .xls files (one measure per file: hand raises → pen lifts, segment width → width, total time, writing time, air time).

  1. Files: choose the files. Each file's measure is recognised from its name or sheet; you can set it by hand. A measure can't come from two files.
  2. Choose the Domain for new children and a Session label (for example "Legacy").
  3. Check files: shows how many children are new or already in the system, the block rows, and a sample. Nothing is written yet.
  4. Import. Each child is matched by their old number or created as LEG-<number>. Importing again under the same label updates the values; nothing is doubled.

The files have no grade, gender, SES, school or birth month: these stay "Not recorded" until an evaluator completes the record. The hand comes from L/R (1 right, 0 left). The unit of the old segment width is not confirmed; it is stored as given. Imports lists every import.

Action log

Every action in the system, newest first; the last 7 days by default.

  • Filters: From / To (with Last 24 hours and Any time), Event types (by area, or a whole area), Severity, User, Session code, Domain / Region and Free text (messages, details and event names). Press Search.
  • Click a row to see its details (user email, record, device, address and the details). Show more loads the next 100.
  • Export to CSV saves the filtered entries (up to 50,000); times are in UTC. The export is itself logged.
  • Severity Security marks failed sign-ins, wrong director PINs and user changes. No password or PIN ever appears in the log.
