User guides

One guide for each kind of user. In the system, the Help button on every screen opens the part of your guide about that screen.

Evaluator guide

Registering participants and consent, planning sessions, pairing the tablet, running the steps, marking intervention sheets, hand video, the Comparative summary and replay. Uses the Evaluator cockpit and the tablet.

Researcher guide

Planning participants and sessions, research sessions, results, the Research Director's approval, and the analyst portal. Uses the Evaluator cockpit and the analyst portal.

System Administrator guide

Users, Domains and Regions, settings, the canvas library and reference recordings, cameras, ethics approvals, legacy data and the action log. Uses the Administrator cockpit.

Analyst guide

The de-identified research data: the Overview charts, filters and cohorts, single sessions, replay, kinematics, paper and legacy results, and exports. Uses the analyst portal.
