Evaluator guide

For evaluators who run ILT sessions with children: registering participants, planning and running sessions on the tablet, marking intervention sheets and reading the results.

Your work in the system

You work in the Evaluator cockpit on a computer, while the child writes on the tablet, the Child station. The cockpit shows what the child writes, live, and lets you send each step of the session to the tablet.

  • Participants are known only by a participant code. Never type a name, ID number or initials anywhere. The school is kept only as a protected code, and only the birth month and year are stored.
  • A session is one meeting with a child, built from a protocol: Day 1 (acquisition), Day 2 (24-hour retention), Day 14 (two-week automaticity) and Intervention sessions with real letters.
  • The child writes in houses: the boxes of the ILT page. A block has 15 houses.
  • The settings a session uses (timers, scoring) are fixed when the session is created. Later setting changes don't alter it.

The usual order of work: register the participant → record consent → plan the session → prepare and pair the tablet → run the steps → read the results.

Signing in

  1. Open the Evaluator cockpit address that your System Administrator gave you (for example https://<server>:5174).
  2. Enter your email and password, and press Sign in. The eye button shows the password as you type it.
  • The interface opens in your own language, set by the administrator. The Language switch at the top changes it for this visit. Theme chooses Light, Dark or Like the computer.
  • After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes. The administrator can unlock it and give you a new password.
  • The Help button at the top of every screen opens the part of this guide about that screen. The small (?) buttons next to some panels open the part about that panel.

The System Administrator signs in at a different address (…/admin). Analysts and researchers also have the analyst portal.

The Sessions screen

After signing in you see your sessions, newest first. Each row shows the session code, the participant code, the session type (with “Research” for a research session), the language, the status and when it was created.

  • Filters: Search (a session or participant code), the status (Planned, Paired, In progress, Paused, Completed, Aborted), the type and the dates From / To. Clear filters shows everything again. The number of sessions found is shown above the list.
  • Open opens the session in the live session screen.
  • Protocol, or the participant code, opens the participant's Dispatch Console.
  • New participant and New session open the forms described below.

A new participant

  1. On Sessions, press New participant.
  2. Fill in:
    • Participant code: the study code from the list the principal investigator keeps outside the system. It can never be changed later, and must be unique (capital and small letters count as the same code).
    • School: typed once and kept only as a protected code; the name is not stored.
    • Birth month and year: the child's age in months is worked out for every session.
    • Domain (the study group), Region (optional), Grade, Gender, Socio-economic group, Dominant hand.
    • Group: Tablet for children who write on the tablet, or Paper cohort for children who write on paper and whose scores you enter by hand (Paper scores).
  3. Press Create. The new participant is chosen in the participant field, ready for a session.

No names, ID numbers or initials, in any field, including notes.

Next, open the participant record to record consent.

A new session

  1. On Sessions, type the participant code in the participant field and choose the participant.
  2. Choose the Session type (Day 1, Day 2 or Day 14; intervention sessions are planned in the Dispatch Console) and the Session language, the language of the child. It decides the tablet's instructions and which canvases are used. Only the languages enabled for the participant's Domain are offered.
  3. Choose the Purpose: Intervention (the default) or Research. Research can be chosen only when research sessions are turned on in the settings, a research ethics approval is valid today, and the participant's consent allows research use. When one is missing, the form says which.
  4. Press Create. The session gets a code (for example S261115-AB2C) and opens in the live session screen.

You can also plan Day 2 and Day 14 from the Dispatch Console (Plan the … session), and intervention sessions from its intervention panel.

The steps of each day come from the protocol template. Day 1: intro video, warm-up block (not scored), ILT blocks 1–4, notebook demo video, notebook pages 1–4 on the tablet, ILT blocks 5–8. Day 2: ILT blocks 1–4 and 2 pages in a real paper notebook, timed by you. Day 14: ILT blocks 1–2. The administrator can turn the warm-up off for a Region.

The participant record

Open it with Participant record next to the participant field on Sessions, or at the top of the Dispatch Console.

  • Details: Domain, Region, school ("Kept as a protected code"), birth month, grade, gender, socio-economic group and dominant hand, with the number of sessions and when the record was created and last changed.
  • Edit changes the details. The participant code can't change. To change the school, type the new school name; leave it empty to keep the current one.
  • Deactivate takes the participant out of the participant search, so no new session can be created for them. Their sessions and results are kept, and their Dispatch Console and summary still open. Reactivate undoes it.
  • Research sessions says whether a research session can be created for this participant, and if not, why.
  • Hand video says whether hand video will be recorded: it needs both the system setting and the participant's consent.

Dispatch Console at the top goes back to the console.

Consent

Consent is recorded on the participant record. With no consent recorded, the most private defaults apply: no hand video, no research use and no open-science sharing.

  1. Press Record consent (or Record a new consent).
  2. Enter the Consent form version and the date it was Signed on (not in the future).
  3. Tick only what the signed form allows: Hand video, Research use, Open-science sharing. Every box starts unticked.
  4. Press Record consent.

A new consent replaces the current one; the earlier ones stay in the Consent history, with who recorded them.

Recording a consent without research use takes the participant's research results out of the analyst portal straight away. Whether a session records hand video is decided when the session is created.

Paper scores

For a participant in the Paper cohort, the record has a Paper scores grid: Day 1 ILT 1–8, Day 2 ILT 1–4 and Day 14 ILT 1–2.

  • For each block written on paper, enter the pen lifts (0–200), the accurate letters (0–15), the time in seconds and any notes, then press Save on that row.
  • Leave a block empty if it was not written. Emptying a saved block and saving removes it.
  • Every save is logged with the old and new values.

Paper blocks appear in the Comparative summary tagged Paper. Paper has no speed, so no speed score or composite.

Results from the old application

Children imported from the previous application have the tag "Legacy (old application)" with their old number. Their record shows Results from the old application: per block, the pen lifts, segment width, total, writing and air time, as imported. They are read-only. The imported files have no grade, gender or school, so these show "Not recorded" until you complete the record with Edit.

The Dispatch Console

The ILT Evaluator Control & Dispatch Console shows one participant's whole protocol on one page. Open it with Protocol on the Sessions screen or in a session.

  • Day 1, Day 2 and Day 14 are panels of step cards. Blocks or pages of one kind that follow each other form one card, for example "ILT 4 Blocks · Blocks 1–4 (Scored)".
  • Card colours: green COMPLETED; blue ACTIVE (with how many of its steps are done); grey DISPATCH (still to run); lighter grey SKIPPED; grey PENDING Day N (the day's session is not planned yet).
  • Clicking a card opens its session in the live session screen, where you send the steps to the tablet.
  • Plan the Day 2 session / Plan the Day 14 session create that day's session.
  • The Day 14 panel shows the two blocks' average score once both are scored, and Comparative Summary View opens the summary.
  • The status bar at the bottom names the step running on the tablet, the timer policy in effect and the warm-up setting. OPEN LIVE COCKPIT opens the session in progress (or the first planned one).
  • A session run by another evaluator is shown with its status, but its cards can't be clicked ("Another evaluator runs this session.").

The intervention program

The green Intervention program panel of the Dispatch Console plans the intervention sessions, one target letter at a time. Only letters that the administrator has activated are offered.

  • Click a letter to make it the Target letter. Each letter shows its state: Not trained, Trained once, Retention from date, Retention due, Retention done.
  • Video + 4 Training Sheets: the first training of the letter: its video (skipped with a note when the library has none), then four letter sheets.
  • Repeat Letter: another training of the same letter.
  • Next Letter: starts training the next letter that has not been trained.
  • 2-Wk Retention: one sheet, offered from 14 days after the letter's last completed training (the number of days is a setting).
  • Purpose: Intervention, or Research when research is allowed (see A new session).

Only one intervention session can be open at a time: finish (or abort) it before planning the next. The planned session appears as a card under Intervention sessions; click it to open it. The card shows the sheets done, the score (the mean composite of the scored sheets), or how many marks are still to set.

Preparing the tablet

  1. Charge the tablet and place it in front of the child, with the pen.
  2. Open the Child station address on the tablet (for example https://<server>:5173). It shows Type the 6-digit code.
  3. The page should fill the screen. If the browser bars are showing, tap the title bar or any area outside the writing page; after the tablet wakes from sleep, a ⛶ Full screen button appears in the corner.
  • The canvas is drawn at true size. The tablet answers the pen only: touches of the hand are ignored.
  • If the network drops, the tablet keeps the writing and sends it when it reconnects ("No connection to the server. Writing is kept on the tablet").
  • The tablet shows its messages in the session language.

Pairing the tablet

  1. In the live session screen, under Tablet pairing, press Show pairing code. A 6-digit code appears, valid for 10 minutes (New code gives another).
  2. On the tablet, type the code and press Connect. The cockpit shows Tablet connected, and the tablet shows "Waiting for the evaluator to start".
  • One tablet serves one session at a time.
  • The tablet stays tied to its session through reloads, network drops and clearing the browser, until the session is finished, aborted or the tablet is released. This protects the child's writing; it is also why a tablet still tied to an old session doesn't take a new code: release it first.
  • Leaving an unfinished session: Back with the tablet connected asks: Release tablet and go back, Go back without releasing or Stay in this session.
  • Release tablet (next to Abort session) does the same from inside the session, also when the tablet is off or out of range. The tablet returns to its code screen. The session stays open: open it again, show a new code and pair to go on where it stopped.
  • You can't release the tablet while a block or a video runs on it: finish or stop it first.
  • A tablet that reconnects after its session was finished or aborted shows the end screen and returns to the code screen by itself.
  • The code is wrong or expired: the tablet says so; ask for a new code. After too many wrong codes the tablet waits 15 minutes.

The live session screen

The header names the participant, the session type, the purpose and the language. The screen has:

  • Tablet pairing and, when cameras are set up, Hand video (camera).
  • The live mirror of the tablet page and the Clinical telemetry panel (see below).
  • Session steps: every step of the session, in order, with its state and result (Running the steps).
  • The session controls: Pause, Finish block manually, Repeat block, Abort session, Release tablet, Finish session (see below).
  • Comparative summary and Protocol (the Dispatch Console).

Running the steps

The big Next step: … button runs the first step that is not done yet, whatever its type. You can also act on each step in Session steps.

Videos

  • Play on tablet plays the video full screen on the tablet in the session language; the cockpit shows "Playing on the tablet". The step is done when the video ends, or when you press Stop video.
  • If the browser blocks the sound, the tablet shows Tap to play the video: tap it.
  • A video that has no version in the session language is flagged: "No video in the session language: skip it and explain verbally". Press Skip and explain to the child yourself.

Writing steps (warm-up, ILT blocks, notebook pages, letter sheets)

  • Send to tablet sends the step. The tablet counts down ("Get ready…"), then shows the page, for example "Block 1 of 8".
  • The child writes the houses in order. A finished or skipped house takes no more ink (forward only). On notebook pages, earlier rows lock once the child moves to the next row.
  • The warm-up is one full block, measured but not scored. Verbal help is allowed.
  • Notebook pages use the same letter, 3 rows of 5, and are timed only.

Paper notebook pages (Day 2)

  • The child writes in a real paper notebook. Press Start when the child starts and Stop when they finish; Continue resumes the timer.
  • Add Notes if needed and press Save page. The step shows the time and the note.

Skipping

Skip marks a step as skipped without running it. Skips are logged.

Live mirror and clinical telemetry

The mirror shows the child's page as it is written. The ink is coloured by pen pressure (light, medium, firm), and the pen's path in the air above the page is drawn in yellow.

The Clinical telemetry panel updates about once a second:

LineMeaning
Active houseThe house being written, for example 6 of 15
Block timeFrom the first pen-down in the first house
InactivityTime without writing, against the threshold
Pen lifts in blockThe norm is 15 at the end of a block

Skipped housesHouses left empty
Average ILT speedIn mm/s

Letter sheets show the time, inactivity, pen lifts and skipped houses only; their marks appear below (Marking intervention sheets).

Inactivity

After 20 s without writing (30 s after the last house) the tablet shows a soft glow around the page. After 10 s more, the telemetry panel turns amber ("No writing: check the child") and, when allowed, Finish block manually blinks amber. The times are settings.

How a block ends

  • Next block on the tablet: after the last house, the tablet shows Next block. It answers a tap of the pen only, and only after the last house. It ends the block and starts the next writing block at once, with no countdown. (A step sent from the cockpit still has the countdown.) On notebook pages it comes on after at least 5 strokes in the last row.
  • Finish block manually: stays faded until the child has written in the last house (on a notebook page: reached the last row) and 30 s have passed without the pen touching the page. Then it blinks amber. If a child stops before the last house, the block can't be ended with Manual finish: use Pause or Abort session.
  • When the block is stored, the cockpit shows "Block saved" with the number of samples and the integrity check, and a result card: time, pen lifts, accuracy, average speed, the speed, accuracy and pen-lift scores and the composite. A warm-up says "measured, not scored"; a notebook page "timed only".
  • The speed score and composite show "not configured" until a normal speed is recorded for the canvas, and Pass/Fail until the pass threshold is set. This is set by the administrator.
  • The tablet asks the child to put the pen down on the table beside the tablet when a block is complete.

Pause, repeat, abort, release, finish

  • Pause (with a Pause reason) shows "Short break" on the tablet; Resume goes on.
  • Repeat block: for a finished block that was disturbed. Choose a Repeat reason and confirm. The finished attempt is kept for audit and the block is sent again; the step shows "1 earlier attempt(s) repeated". The latest finished attempt is the one that counts.
  • Abort session ends the session early. The data collected so far is kept.
  • Release tablet: frees the tablet; the session stays open (see Pairing).
  • Finish session: when all steps are done or skipped. The tablet shows "All done! Well done" and returns to its code screen. A finished session also releases its camera.
  • If the tablet restarts during a block, the block is stopped: "The tablet restarted during the block…". Send it to the tablet again.

Marking intervention sheets

A letter sheet has three rows of five letters:

RowThe childCorrect whenWho decides
1Traces over the model through the dotsEvery dot touched in order and no pen lift inside the letter (ה ק ח ת א may lift the pen once between strokes)The system suggests
2Writes from the start dot onlyStarts at the start dot and the form is rightThe system checks the start; you confirm the form
3Writes freehandRecognisably the letter, right stroke direction, starting about where row 2 startedYou
  • The marks panel shows each row as soon as the child moves on to the next row. The letters sit as on the sheet (right to left for Hebrew).
  • Rows 1 and 2 come with the system's suggestion (✓ / ✗, "system"). Change only what you disagree with: your mark shows "evaluator". Pressing your own choice again returns the mark to the system's suggestion.
  • Row 3 starts empty, with dashed frames "to set". Each letter shows a similarity hint (0–100): a hint only, it never counts.
  • You can set marks during the sheet or later: Marks on a sheet in Session steps opens its marks again.
  • The sheet is scored once every mark is set: accuracy (correct marks ÷ 15), the pen-lift and speed scores and the composite. Until then it says how many marks are left. Changing a mark later scores the sheet again.

Hand video (camera)

When hand video is allowed (the system setting and the participant's consent, when the session was created) and cameras are set up, the session screen has a Hand video panel. Otherwise it says the video is off for this session.

  1. Pair the tablet first, and make sure no step is running.
  2. Press Pair a camera. The tablet shows a large QR code ("Camera pairing"), valid for 3 minutes.
  3. Point a camera at the tablet. Within a few seconds the camera that sees the code is paired: the cockpit shows Camera: name and opens the live view; the tablet shows a frame.
  4. Turn or move the camera until the whole frame and the child's writing hand are in view. A camera that can turn has arrow buttons (Up, Down, Left, Right). Rotate 180° turns the picture if the camera faces the child.
  5. Press Done. The tablet returns to its waiting screen.
  • Each writing step is then recorded as its own clip, video only, no sound. The panel shows ● Recording while a clip runs; the camera can't be released then.
  • Position the camera opens the live view again; Release camera frees it for another session.
  • A camera serves one session at a time, so sessions side by side never mix cameras. "No free camera is ready" means every camera is busy or not working: ask the administrator.

The Comparative summary

Open it with Comparative summary in a session or Comparative Summary View in the Dispatch Console. It compares five anchors:

  • Day 1 · first pair (blocks 1–2), Day 1 · last pair (7–8), Day 2 · first pair (1–2), Day 2 · last pair (3–4), Day 14 (1–2).
  • Each box shows the average time, pen lifts (a whole number), accuracy %, average ILT speed, the speed, accuracy and pen-lift scores, the composite and Pass/Fail, with a replay button for each block.
  • The composite weights and the pass threshold in effect are shown at the top. "not configured" means the threshold, or the canvas's normal speed, has not been set yet.
  • Blocks entered from paper are tagged Paper: no replay, speed or composite.
  • The Comparative summary diagram draws the measures across the five anchors; point at an anchor to see its values.
  • Below: the Intervention program table (one row per intervention session, with its scored sheets) and, for imported children, Results from the previous application, as imported and not scored.

Replay

Replay (on a result card, a step or a summary box) plays a block back like a video.

  • Play / Pause, the Timeline to jump, the Speed from 0.25× to 4×, and one sample back or forward.
  • The speed of each letter is shown under its house. Ink is coloured by pressure; the in-air path is yellow.
  • If hand video was recorded, it plays beside the page, following play, pause, the timeline and the speed. Rotate 180° turns it (remembered in this browser).

You can replay the sessions you ran.
